None of this is a loophole. It's all in the tax code.

Most business owners never hear about these. Here are a few we use all the time. Your plan may include more.

Hire Your Kids

Pay your kids real wages for real work. The business deducts it, and up to the standard deduction ($16,100 for 2026) they may owe no income tax on it.

Good fit if your kids can do real work in the business.

Augusta Rule

Rent your home to your business for up to 14 days a year for real business use. The business deducts it; the rent can be tax-free to you.

Good fit if you hold meetings or events at home.

Solo 401(k)

Put away far more than an IRA allows, pre-tax or Roth.

Good fit if you're self-employed with no full-time staff besides a spouse.

Accountable Plan

Your business reimburses real business costs like home office, phone and mileage. It deducts them, and you don't pay income tax on them.

Good fit if you run an S-Corp or C-Corp.

Health Savings Account

Deductible going in, tax-free growth, tax-free out for medical costs.

Good fit if you're on a qualifying high-deductible plan.

Health Reimbursement Arrangement

Your business pays medical costs with pre-tax dollars instead of you paying with after-tax dollars.

Good fit if you can meet HRA eligibility rules.

Traditional & Roth IRA

Pick the right account in the right year; the lifetime difference can be big.

Good fit for almost everyone.

529 Education Plan

Tax-free growth and tax-free qualified withdrawals, plus a state deduction in many states.

Good fit if you're saving for school.
