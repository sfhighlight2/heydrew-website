Tax strategy FAQ: straight answers

This tax strategy FAQ answers what owners ask us most. HeyDrew is a tax strategy service for business owners, powered by AI and backed by licensed professionals. Below: how it works, what using it is like, taxes and filing, pricing, and bookkeeping and payroll.

How does HeyDrew work?

2 answers

HeyDrew plans your taxes forward: a short assessment, a call about fit and pricing, then a Tax Valet who builds your strategy with licensed professionals.

Q1

What is HeyDrew?

Hey! We’re a tax strategy service for business owners, powered by AI, backed by licensed pros. Most accountants look backward at a year that’s already over. We look forward, at the moves you can still make.

Q2

How does it actually work?

Five steps:

  1. Answer a few quick questions about your business.
  2. Book a call: we’ll talk through your situation, fit, and pricing.
  3. Meet your Tax Valet, your dedicated point of contact.
  4. Send over your documents.
  5. Get your strategy, plus a plan to actually implement it.

How HeyDrew works, step by step

What is it like to use HeyDrew?

7 answers

You work with one Tax Valet through one secure portal, and your information stays with the people on your account.

Q3

What do you need from me to get started?

Prior-year returns, your entity details, and a picture of how money moves through your business. Your Tax Valet sends an exact checklist. No guessing.

The first-call checklist

Q4

How do I reach my Tax Valet?

Message them right from your portal. You’ll hear back within a couple of hours, usually sooner.

What a Tax Valet does

Q5

Where do my documents live?

In your secure client portal. Upload once, and your whole team can see what they need.

Q6

Is my information secure?

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with access limited to the people actually working your account. Your Tax Valet and the tax professionals on your account. Nobody else.

Security and privacy at HeyDrew

Q7

Do you sell my data?

Never. We don’t sell it, rent it, or share it with advertisers. And we won’t use or disclose your tax information beyond preparing your return without your written consent.

How we protect your tax data

Q8

What if I don’t submit anything this year?

We don’t cancel on you. We roll your engagement into the following year.

Q9

What kinds of businesses is this for?

S-Corps, LLCs, partnerships, and self-employed folks with real complexity: multiple income streams, property, equipment, or a growing team.

Tax planning for S-Corp owners

How does HeyDrew handle taxes and filing?

6 answers

HeyDrew builds a custom strategy from the rules you qualify for, helps you document it, and offers filing through licensed professionals.

Q10

Do you file my return, too?

Yes, filing is available. Licensed professionals handle everything that requires a license.

Q11

Can you guarantee I’ll save money?

No, and be skeptical of anyone who says otherwise. What we guarantee is that we’ll look at every strategy you legitimately qualify for and show you the math.

Q12

Will these strategies raise my audit risk?

We only recommend strategies you actually qualify for, and we help you build the documentation to back them up. Defensibility is the whole point.

How to keep records that hold up

Q13

What if I’m behind on prior years?

We can help you catch up. Bring it up on your call.

Q14

I have multiple entities and multiple states. Can you handle that?

Yes. Tell us the full picture on your call so we scope it right.

Q15

Is my strategy custom, or a template?

Custom. It’s built from your numbers, your entity structure, and your goals.

Tax strategies for business owners, explained

How does HeyDrew pricing work?

5 answers

Pricing is one annual fee, set by your entities and complexity and quoted on your call before you commit.

Q16

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your entities and complexity, so we cover it live on your call. No surprises, no fine print. You’ll know the full number before you commit to anything.

How pricing works

Q17

Why isn’t pricing on the site?

Because a single number would be wrong for most people who read it. Five minutes on a call gets you a real answer instead of a range.

Q18

How is it billed?

Annual, paid upfront.

Q19

What happens when my year is up?

We reach out about 30 days before your renewal with your options.

Q20

What’s included?

Your Tax Valet, your strategy build, implementation support, and year-round access. Your call covers exactly what’s in your program.

What pricing covers

Does HeyDrew do bookkeeping and payroll?

6 answers

Yes. Bookkeeping and payroll are optional, and the same Tax Valet who runs your strategy coordinates them.

Q21

Do you do more than tax strategy?

We do. Bookkeeping and payroll both live under the same roof, with the same Tax Valet coordinating.

Bookkeeping and payroll for business owners

Q22

Why would I add bookkeeping?

Because strategy runs on clean books. If your categorization is a mess, so is every number we’re planning around. We handle the monthly work so the strategy holds up.

Q23

Can you run my payroll?

Yes, with an in-house payroll specialist. That matters most if you’re an S-Corp owner: paying yourself the right way is a strategy decision, not just a paperwork one.

Q24

Do I have to add these to work with HeyDrew?

Nope. Tax strategy stands on its own. Add bookkeeping or payroll whenever it makes sense. Mid-year is fine.

Q25

What do they cost?

Depends on your volume and how many entities you’re running. Your Tax Valet scopes it and sends you an invoice directly. No packages you have to decode.

Q26

Who actually does the work?

Dedicated bookkeeping and payroll specialists. Your Tax Valet stays your single point of contact, so you’re not chasing three inboxes.
