Making money is the first game. Keeping it is the second.

I lost everything in 2008. Rebuilding taught me something nobody tells you when you start a business: making money is the first game. Keeping it is the second one, and most owners never get coached on it.

For years I've built tax plans for families with serious wealth. The strategies aren't secret. They're in the tax code. Most owners just never get shown them, because their CPA is busy looking at last year.

HeyDrew brings that playbook to every business owner ready to use it.
