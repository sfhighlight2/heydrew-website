# HeyDrew Homepage: Conversion Strategy

**For:** HeyDrew marketing and development teams · **From:** CMO, with the CRO, UI/UX design and brand strategy leads · **Date:** 2026-09-24
**Build spec:** `BLUEPRINT.md` (tokens, copy, wireframes, events). This document explains *why*.

---

## 1. The short version

The current homepage asks visitors for a sensitive financial commitment (the TaxLand quiz, which opens by asking for revenue) before it has earned trust or explained what happens next. It also weighs about 7.4 MB. The redesign reverses the order: **one clear promise, the product shown on the first screen, proof and honest terms before the ask, and one green button that always does the same thing.**

- **Primary goal:** more visitors start and finish the TaxLand assessment.
- **Secondary goal:** visitors who aren't ready for a quiz can book a strategy call.
- **Look and feel:** premium fintech (Ramp, Mercury, Pilot), with Drew as a guide at four moments instead of the backdrop for the whole page.
- **Guardrail:** we measure through to booked calls and closed plans, so a variant can't "win" by sending worse leads.

## 2. What's wrong with the current page

| # | Problem | What visitors experience | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The headline doesn't say who it's for.** "Keep Your Money, Stop Overpaying the IRS" could be TurboTax or a debt-relief ad. | S-Corp and LLC owners don't recognize themselves; W-2 visitors start the quiz and bounce. | Wrong people in, right people out. |
| 2 | **The ask comes before the trust.** "Take The Assessment" has no time, cost or next step, and no proof is visible on the first screen. | "Is this a sales form? How long is it? Who are these people?" | Hesitation at the one moment that matters. |
| 3 | **The header points the wrong way.** The green button is "Sign In" (existing clients). "Get Started" goes to a TaxLand *login* page, while body buttons go to the TaxLand start. | 4 | **The proof undermines itself.** "$135K", "$50K" and "100% Success Rate" have no asterisk; the disclaimer is in a collapsed footer drawer. Meanwhile the FAQ says "be skeptical of anyone who guarantees savings." | Sophisticated owners read the stats as hype, and the page contradicts its best trust line. | Trust and compliance risk. |
| 5 | **Testimonials are vague and hidden.** Three of six are behind a carousel; several read like event feedback; headshot filenames don't match names. | Weak social proof. | Missed reassurance. |
| 6 | **No credentials, no pricing terms, no "what happens next."** Great answers exist in the FAQ ("never auto-renews", "you'll know the full price before you commit") but none are on the homepage. | Unanswered objections at decision time. | Drop-off. |
| 7 | **It looks like a storybook.** A full-width cartoon valley and three large Drews set a consumer-game price anchor for a service with an annual fee. | "Is this serious enough to trust with my business taxes?" | Lower perceived expertise and willingness to pay. |
| 8 | **It's slow and inaccessible.** ~7.4 MB (Vimeo, GIFs, video loops); peers ship 0.25–0.5 MB. Zoom is disabled. White text on the green button is 2.14:1 contrast (fails). | Slow first paint on mobile; hard-to-read buttons. | Bounce, especially from paid mobile traffic. |
| 9 | **TaxLand's first step is the hardest one.** It opens on revenue, and on mobile a cookie banner covers half the question. | Friction spike at the moment of commitment. | Quiz abandonment. |

## 3. The strategy

### 3.1 Positioning
**Category:** year-round tax *strategy*, set against tax *prep*. Tax prep records what already happened; HeyDrew works on the part of the year you can still change.
**Promise:** we find every legitimate strategy you qualify for, help you set it up during the year, and show you the math.
**Headline:** "You built the income. Now keep it." It's the brand's own line tightened, ties to Andrew's Create / **Keep** / Multiply, flatters the reader and makes no numeric promise. The sub names the audience (business owners) and the mechanism (AI finds, licensed CPAs set up and file).
**Wedge against competitors:** filing tools (Keeper, Collective) look backward; tax-planning software (Instead, TaxPlanIQ) is sold to CPA firms, not owners; seminar-style firms (Anderson, Tax Hive) lead with guarantees and gate everything behind a sales call. HeyDrew is the only one with a free self-serve entry (TaxLand), a strategy-first plan, in-year implementation, licensed filing, a founder with an audience, and an honest "no, we can't guarantee savings."

### 3.2 Four principles behind every decision
1. **Earn before you ask.** Proof, credentials and terms sit close to every CTA.
2. **Make the first step easy.** The first question is a one-tap "how is your business set up?", not revenue.
3. **Honest numbers only.** Every figure is labeled illustrative with its disclaimer on screen. Hype stats are gone.
4. **One action, one color.** Green means "See what you can keep." Nothing else is green except money gains and checkmarks.

### 3.3 The big idea: the hero *is* the product
The first screen shows a "Your 2026 Strategy Plan" card with strategy rows still unchecked. The visitor answers two tap questions (entity type, revenue range), and the card unlocks "Good news. Strategies like these may fit a business like yours." with the matched strategies checked (no strategy counts anywhere, by client rule: HeyDrew keeps adding strategies) then hands off to TaxLand with those answers pre-filled so the visitor arrives about 30% done. No email, no dollar figure, no call required. This combines Collective's "How much can I save?" pattern, Keeper and Pilot's product-card heroes, and quiz-funnel mechanics (easy first question, progress already underway).

## 5. CTA strategy

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Primary label** | "See what you can keep" on every green button (mobile header: "Start free"). It matches TaxLand's own start screen, so the promise and the destination agree. Retired: "Take The Assessment", "Get Started", "Unlock Your Savings Now!", "Save My Money". |
| **Microcopy** | "Free · Takes a few minutes · No credit card." (We'll add a real time once TaxLand's median completion time is measured.) |
| **Destination** | Always `taxland.heydrew.com/` with `src`, `pos`, UTMs and the GA cross-domain linker. From the estimator, also `entity` and `rev`. Never `/login`. |
| **Design** | Brand green gradient with dark navy text (8.66:1 contrast, up from 2.14:1), 12px radius, 52px tall in key spots, 56px full-width on mobile. |
| **Frequency** | Header, hero, how it works, strategies, example, TaxLand portal, pricing, final band, plus the mobile sticky bar. Same words every time. |
| **Secondary** | "Book a strategy call": a text link in the hero, a ghost button in the team, pricing and final sections, a link under the FAQ. Never green, never bigger than the primary. |
| **Mobile sticky bar** | Appears once the hero card scrolls away; hides over sections that already show a CTA. If a visitor answered question 1 but not 2, it reads "Finish your plan · 1 question left". |
| **Existing clients** | "Sign in" text link in the header and footer. |

## 6. Changes needed outside the homepage (TaxLand)
These multiply the homepage's impact. The homepage launches without them (extra URL parameters are ignored harmlessly), but test T1 needs items 1–2.
1. Accept `entity` and `rev` parameters, skip answered questions, open on the next one.
2. Start the progress bar partly filled.
3. Stop the cookie banner from covering the question on mobile.
4. Ask for email/phone at the results reveal, not at the start.
5. Offer call booking on the results screen.
6. Retire `/login` as an entry point for prospects.

## 7. KPIs

**North star:** qualified TaxLand lead submits per homepage session.

| Funnel stage | Metric | Event(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Engage | Estimator Q1 rate (Q1 answers ÷ hero views) | `estimator_q1_answer` / `hero_view` |
| Start | Homepage → TaxLand click-through | `cta_click` (primary) / sessions |
| Arrive | TaxLand landings with prefill | `taxland_landing{prefilled}` |
| Complete | TaxLand completion + drop-off by question | `taxland_step_n`, `taxland_lead_submit` |
| Talk | Calls booked (homepage + TaxLand results) | `call_booked{source}` |
| **Guardrails** | Call-held rate, close rate, plan value (CRM import) | `call_held`, `closed_won`, `plan_value` |
| Health | LCP ≤2.0s mobile, CLS ≤0.05, page ≤1.2 MB | Web Vitals |
| Diagnostic | FAQ opens by question, sticky bar clicks, scroll depth, strategy card opens | `faq_open`, `sticky_cta_click`, `scroll_depth`, `strategy_card_open` |

**Baseline first:** instrument the current page for 2 weeks before launch so every change is measured against HeyDrew's own numbers. We won't set targets from vendor benchmarks; for context only, Unbounce reports a 6.6% median landing-page conversion rate, and quiz tools report much higher start-to-lead rates for a different event.

## 8. A/B test roadmap
Run one at a time, to a sample size fixed in advance (95% significance), judged on the north star with the guardrails intact.

| # | Test | Hypothesis | Primary metric | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T0** | New page vs current page (50/50 split) | The redesign lifts qualified lead submits without hurting call-held rate. | `taxland_lead_submit` / session | Baseline done |
| **T1** | Estimator hero vs plain-CTA hero | A two-tap estimator that pre-fills TaxLand raises lead submits by 15%+ because the first step is easy and the visitor arrives partly done. | `taxland_lead_submit` / session | TaxLand items 1–2 |
| **T2** | Result shows strategy count vs count + illustrative $ range | Specific dollar ranges may pull harder, or may read as hype to skeptical owners. | Handoff click rate; lead quality | Legal + CPA approval of ranges |
| **T3** | Mobile sticky bar with "1 question left" resume state vs plain sticky | Reminding partial completers lifts mobile TaxLand starts by 8%+. | Mobile `cta_click` → `taxland_landing` | – |
| **T4** | Headline: "You built the income. Now keep it." vs "Pay what you owe. Not a dollar more." | The legitimacy frame may convert skeptical owners better. | `estimator_q1_answer` / `hero_view`, then lead submits | – |
| **T5** | Header label "See what you can keep" vs "Start free assessment" | Naming "free" and "assessment" may lift header clicks. | Header `cta_click` | – |
| **T6** | Hero Drew pointing vs Drew holding phone vs no Drew | Tests whether the guide helps or cheapens the premium feel. | Q1 rate; lead quality | Phone screen retouch |
| **T7** | Deadline bar on vs off (Q4 only) | True urgency lifts starts without raising bounce. | Lead submits in Oct–Dec | Legal-approved wording |
| Backlog | Testimonial order (money outcome first) · desktop exit-intent strategy checklist PDF for email · founder video placement | – | – | – |

## 9. Compliance commitments
- Every dollar figure is labeled illustrative, with its disclaimer on screen and the full text expanded in the footer.
- No guarantees, no "100% success", no invented testimonials, client logos, press logos, ratings or team members.
- The six real testimonials are used verbatim. The Trustpilot 4.9 rating belongs to andrewcordle.com, so it appears only if Andrew approves and only attributed to him.
- The estimator shows a preliminary strategy screen, not tax advice, and its rules are signed off by a HeyDrew CPA.
